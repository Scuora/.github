### Most organisations don’t lack software. They lack software that fits together.

Admissions live in a spreadsheet. Money lives in bank slips. People live in group chats. Decisions come from whatever someone remembered to export.

**Scuora Solutions Limited** is a Nairobi technology company that exists to close those gaps. We advise organisations on technology and engineer the systems they need, from a single integration to a complete operational platform, and we support them long after launch. We also build and run software products of our own, which keeps our engineering honest.

## Engineering principles

<sub>How we work, not certifications or guarantees.</sub>

| | | |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Clear architecture**<br><sub>Understandable, changeable, extendable.</sub> | **Usable & accessible**<br><sub>Designed for real users, devices and conditions.</sub> | **Secure & private**<br><sub>Least privilege, protected data, privacy by design.</sub> |
| **Reliable workflows**<br><sub>Complete correctly, or fail visibly.</sub> | **Risk-led testing**<br><sub>Deepest where errors cost most.</sub> | **Right-sized performance**<br><sub>Designed for the use case, then measured.</sub> |
| **Observable**<br><sub>Monitored, traced and recoverable.</sub> | **Documented**<br><sub>Never dependent on one person’s memory.</sub> | **Thoughtful integration**<br><sub>Respectful of the systems you rely on.</sub> |

> *“A check that could not run is not a clean bill of health.”*
